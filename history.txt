The very first FPGA was the XC2064, created by Xilinx in 1985. It was very primitive, with just 800 gates.


When FPGAs came along, a single device could replace many discrete gate components, lowering costs, saving component space on the circuit board, 
and allowing the design to be reprogrammed as the requirements of the project changed.
